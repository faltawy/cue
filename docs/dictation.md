# Dictation and dictionary

With dictation you speak into any text field on your Mac, and Cue types what you said when you finish. The dictionary teaches Cue names and words it should spell right.

## Dictation

### How do I turn on dictation?

1. Open **Settings → Dictation**.
2. Turn on **Enable dictation**. Cue must stay open for the shortcut to work in other apps.
3. Open **Settings → Permissions** and check that Microphone and Accessibility are allowed. Cue needs Accessibility to type into other apps.

### How do I dictate?

1. Click into a text field in any app.
2. Hold ⌃⌥Space (Control-Option-Space) and speak.
3. Let go. The text appears in the field.

Press Esc to cancel. A small panel shows while Cue is listening.

If you turn on **Hands-free dictation**, press the shortcut once to start and again to finish.

Some limits:

- Dictation stops after five minutes.
- The global dictation shortcut pauses while Cue records a meeting. The microphone button in the [coach](coach.md#how-do-i-ask-the-coach-something) still works during recording.
- Cue doesn't type into password fields.
- If you click into a different field while you speak, Cue doesn't type the text. Copy it from **Last dictation** in **Settings → Dictation**.

### Dictation settings

| Setting | What it does | Default |
| ------- | ------------ | ------- |
| Dictation shortcut | Click it and press new keys (Esc cancels). Use Control, Option, Shift or Command, and pick keys that aren't the coach shortcut. You can also use **Fn / Globe** or **Right Command** alone. | ⌃⌥Space |
| Microphone | The microphone to use. It's the same setting as in **Settings → Meetings**. | Current default |
| Live transcript preview | Shows words in the small panel while you speak, if your transcription service supports it. Cue still types the text when you finish. | Off |
| Hands-free dictation | Press to start and press again to finish, instead of holding the keys | Off |

Use the **Try dictation** box to test. **Last dictation** shows what you said last, with **Copy last dictation**.

Dictation uses the transcription model, languages and dictionary you set on the other settings pages. Cue deletes the recording after it is transcribed. Your last dictation stays in memory until you turn dictation off or quit Cue.

## Dictionary

In **Settings → Dictionary** you list words for Cue to prefer, such as names of people on your team, short forms, company terms and product names.

- Type a word and click **Add**. To add several at once, paste them with commas or line breaks between them.
- Point at a word to edit or remove it.
- The box also searches your list.

Cue gives these words to your transcription service for meetings, re-transcriptions and dictation. Your selected AI provider also gets them when it writes titles and summaries. Cue skips words shorter than two characters and words already in the list.
