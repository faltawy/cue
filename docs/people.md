# Contacts

Cue keeps a list of the people and organizations you meet. Open it from **Settings → Contacts**, or click a participant in a note.

## Where do contacts come from?

- Calendar invites. When Cue reads a meeting from your calendar, it adds the people invited as the note's participants. It makes a contact for each new email address and guesses the name and company from the email if needed.
- You. Click **+** in the contact list and type a name. Or type a name in a note's **Add participants** field and choose **Add "…"**.
- The coach, when you ask it to add or update a contact.

Your own contact is always first in the list. To change your name, use **Your name** in **Settings → General**.

## A person's page

A person's page has Name, Job Title, Company, Email, Phone, LinkedIn and Notes. Click **Add email** to add more email addresses. Cue saves changes as you type.

- Company links the person to an organization. Search for one, or type a new name and choose **Create**.
- Summary. Once the person has been in at least one meeting, Cue uses your selected AI provider to write a summary of your meetings and notes with them.
- Related Notes lists the notes they took part in. You can search them by title.
- The **⋯** menu has **Pin**, **Remove photo** and **Delete**. Click the photo to change it.

## An organization's page

An organization's page shows its name, its photo, and everyone whose Company is set to it. Each person has buttons to email them or open their LinkedIn page.

## Find, sort and pin

- Search by name, email or phone. ⌘F jumps to the search box. Organizations match by name.
- Sort A-Z, Z-A, Oldest or Newest.
- Pin people or organizations to keep them at the top. Drag pinned ones to change their order.

## Merge duplicates

When two contacts share an email or a name, the person's page says "Duplicate Contact Found". Click **Merge** to combine them. Cue moves their notes and transcript lines to one contact and keeps the other email as an extra email.

## Delete a contact

Right-click a contact, or choose **⋯ → Delete**. Cue deletes it at once and shows **Undo** for 10 seconds. This works for people and organizations.

## Participants on a note

The participants of a note are under **⋯ → Meeting info**.

- Type to add someone from your contacts, or a new name.
- Click a participant to open their contact.
- Click a participant's × to remove them. This also removes them as a speaker in the transcript.
- Click **Enhance contact** to let your selected AI provider fill in a participant's details.
