# Updates

Cue finds and downloads new versions by itself. The update installs when you choose to restart Cue, and never in the middle of a meeting.

## How do updates arrive?

Cue checks for a new version 30 seconds after it opens and then every 6 hours. When it finds one, it downloads it in the background. Nothing changes until you restart.

## How do I install an update?

When the download is ready, you see it in two places:

- **Settings → General → Updates** shows "Update ready" with a **Restart to update** button.
- The menu bar menu shows **Restart to Update to** and the new version.

Click either one. Cue installs the update and opens again.

Cue doesn't restart while you record or while the coach is still answering. It tells you why ("Cue restarts once the recording stops." or "Cue restarts once the coach finishes answering."). Try again when that's done.

## How do I check for updates now?

Open **Settings → General → Updates** and click **Check for updates**. The same row shows your version and when Cue last checked, for example "Up to date · checked about 2 hours ago".

## What's new

After an update, Cue opens What's new once to show what changed. To open it later, choose **Help → What's New** in the menu bar, or type "What's new" in ⌘K.

The [releases page](https://github.com/faltawy/cue/releases) lists the changes in every version.

## After an update, macOS asks for my password

macOS may ask for your login password the first time the new version reads the API keys Cue saved in your Keychain. Enter it and choose **Always Allow**. See [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md#macos-asks-for-my-password-after-an-update).

## Install by hand

You can always download the latest version from the [releases page](https://github.com/faltawy/cue/releases/latest) and drag it into Applications to replace the old one. Your notes and settings stay as they are.
