# Templates

A template decides how Cue lays out a meeting summary: which sections it has and what goes in each. Open templates from **Settings → Templates**.

## Auto

**Auto** is first in the list. With Auto, Cue picks a layout for each meeting. You can change how it writes:

- Edit the instructions under **Summary format**, then click **Save**.
- Click **Improve with examples** and attach up to three past summaries you like, as Markdown or text files. Cue changes the format to match them. Cue uses the examples only for this and doesn't keep them.
- Choose **⋯ → Reset to default format** to undo your changes.

Auto shows "Customized" after you change it.

## Built-in templates

Cue comes with ready-made templates. You can use, change or delete them like your own:

1:1 Meeting, Board Meeting, Brainstorming Session, Client Kickoff Meeting, Customer Discovery Interview, Daily Standup, Executive Briefing, Incident Postmortem, Investor Pitch Meeting, Lecture Notes, Performance Review, Product Roadmap Review, Project Kickoff, Sales Discovery Call, Sprint Planning, Sprint Retrospective, Technical Design Review.

## How do I make a template?

1. In **Settings → Templates**, click **+** (or **New template**).
2. Add a title. You can also add an icon, a description and tags.
3. Click **Add Section** for each part of the summary. Give each section a title and describe what goes in it.
4. Drag sections to change their order, or use the section menu: **Insert above**, **Insert below**, **Move up**, **Move down**, **Delete**.

Cue saves your changes as you go.

## Copy, favorite or delete a template

Use the template's **⋯** menu, or right-click it in the list:

- **Duplicate** makes a copy with "(Copy)" in its name.
- **Favorite** (the heart) puts the template at the top of every template list.
- **Delete** asks first, then removes the template for good. Notes already written with it keep their content.

You can search the list by title, description or tag. ⌥↑ and ⌥↓ move to the previous or next template.

## How do I choose a template?

- For every meeting: Open a template and click **Set as default**. Click **Current default** again to go back to Auto.
- For one meeting: Click the summary tab in the note and pick a template. Cue writes the summary again with it. The picker also has **Regenerate**, **Create new template** and **See all templates**.
- Before the meeting: In the Memos tab of an empty note, choose a template under **Start with a favorite template** or **Suggested templates**. Cue then uses it for the summary.
